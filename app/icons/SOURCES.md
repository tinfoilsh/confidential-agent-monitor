# Icon sources

Brand SVGs in this directory are local copies of icon files from the n8n `n8n-nodes-base` and `n8n-nodes-langchain` packages. They were reduced to inert SVG vector elements and internal references. The original package-relative paths are listed below. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
The Grok marks are copied unchanged from the official xAI logo archive linked by [xAI brand guidelines](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines).

The generic `custom.svg`, `email.svg`, `mcp.svg`, `ssh.svg`, `web.svg`, `api.svg`, and `browser.svg` files are original vectors drawn for this app.

Source project: [n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n).
